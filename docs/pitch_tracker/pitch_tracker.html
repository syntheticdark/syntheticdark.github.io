我是汤姆。

Wǒ (3) • shì (4) • Tāng (1) • mǔ (3)

💡 Target Tonal Guide:
  • 我 (Wǒ): Falling-rising profile. Look for a "U" shape dip at the beginning.
  • 是 (shì): High-to-low falling tone. Look for a steep downward ski-slope line.
  • 汤 (Tāng): High flat tone. Look for a high, steady horizontal segment.
  • 姆 (mǔ): Another dipping curve at the end.
